# PhotoLex

**Find any photo by the text visible inside it — 100% offline, 100% private.**

Ever taken a photo of an ID card, a receipt, a whiteboard, or a screenshot — and then lost it among thousands of other photos? PhotoLex finds it for you. Type a word you remember seeing in the photo, and PhotoLex searches the *actual text inside your photos* to find it, entirely on your device.

## How it works

PhotoLex uses on-device text recognition (OCR) to scan your photo gallery in the background, then builds a private, searchable index stored locally on your phone. No photo, no extracted text, and no search query ever leaves your device.

## Key features

- **Search photos by their text** — find ID cards, documents, screenshots, signs, whiteboards, anything with visible text
- **100% offline and private** — no internet connection required, no cloud, no account, no ads, no analytics
- **Background indexing** — scanning continues even when the app is closed
- **Multi-language OCR** — supports Latin-script languages and Devanagari (Hindi and related scripts)
- **Full gallery experience** — PhotoLex is also a complete, modern photo gallery app, not just a search tool

See [`docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md`](docs/PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for the full privacy policy.

## Built on Tulsi Gallery

PhotoLex is a fork of [Tulsi Gallery](https://github.com/AKS-Labs/Tulsi) (AKS-Labs), which is itself a fork of [LavenderPhotos](https://github.com/kaii-lb/LavenderPhotos) (kaii-lb). Both are open source, and PhotoLex is deeply grateful to both projects — this app would not exist without their work on offline OCR gallery search and the underlying gallery experience.
